How it is calculated

  • Net sales = net_sales_idr (gross minus discount), returns included as negative.
  • Month on month: the last month in the data against the month before. Per trading day divides by Monday to Saturday days in each month.
  • Top outlet: the outlet that moved most in the direction of the total change. "Without top outlet" repeats the comparison without it.
  • Region bridge: the change per region; the top outlet is split out of its region. The steps add up to the total change exactly.
  • Weakest outlets: outlet net sales divided by the median of outlets of the same type in the chosen period and filters. Only outlets below their median are listed.
  • Outlets that only appear on out-of-sequence invoices are still counted; the data note is context to check with an admin, not a reason to hide them.
